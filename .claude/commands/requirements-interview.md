# Requirements Interview

Interview me to extract requirements before any feature work. Ask one question at a
time. Cover, in order: who the user is, the concrete problem, what "done" looks like,
data volume and load for year one, what must never break (privacy/security/funds),
and trade-offs. Do not propose a design until every section has a committed answer
("I don't know yet" must be resolved or accepted with a stated default). Summarize the
requirements as a short numbered list, confirm, then save to `docs/requirements/` and
record the decision per the working loop in `CLAUDE.md`.
