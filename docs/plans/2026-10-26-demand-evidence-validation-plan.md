## Research summary — why the plan looks like this

- **Evidence power ranking** for judges: signed LOI > association letter of support > pilot-interest list with real names > documented outreach pipeline > nothing. Cold outreach converts ~5–10%, so we send 8, follow up once, and bank fallback evidence regardless.
- **Deduction 3 does not require fieldwork.** Every headline claim already has an official published anchor — done, see `docs/REALITY_ANCHORS.md`. Impact credibility is now "anchored + pilot-designed", not "simulated".
- **MFIs have a reason to answer:** 724 MRA-licensed MFIs face board-mandated digitization; MFI–fintech pilots are precedented (BRAC; MicroSave report); Bangladesh Bank publishes pilot/partnership guidance. The ask must cost them nothing: one 20-min call about a **shadow pilot on anonymized historical data**.
- **A named human exists for the merchant side:** the **Karwan Bazar Wholesale Kitchen Market Traders Association** (acting president **Firoz Alam**, named in 2026 press by The Financial Express and bdnews24). Associations respond to recognition + a problem statement. The ask is a **letter of support**, not a commitment.
- **The bKash comp strengthens the pitch for free:** bKash already runs a digital merchant loan up to BDT 50,000 — exactly the solo-MFS baseline KendraFi's backtest beats. Cite it, don't fight it.
